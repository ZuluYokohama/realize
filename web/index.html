Don’t take the AI’s word for it.

When ChatGPT or any other model gives you an answer for work, realize is a second check that the AI cannot talk into a yes. If the answer is right, you get a hold. If it is wrong, you get a fail. If nobody can tell yet, it says so.

Spreadsheet formula, picture edit, shared code — if an AI proposed it and you need to know whether it actually holds, that is when you use realize.

You have a job

Something that has a right and a wrong: pick the larger number, recolor a picture, agree on one code.

The AI proposes

A formula, a sequence of steps, a guess. Confident. Maybe correct. Maybe not.

realize checks

A separate program on your computer inspects the proposal. It does not work for the AI. This webpage only shows the result.

Why this exists

The contractor says the house is finished. The inspector does not work for the contractor. That is the benefit: catch an AI mistake before it becomes your mistake.

What it will not pretend

  1. It does not understand every sentence in English.
  2. The AI does not get to grade its own homework.
  3. Wanting a nicer answer does not change the check.
  4. This webpage is a display. The check runs on your computer.
