# Relational certificates (finite kernel)

This is not a dressing-field solver. It is the **certificate pattern** a relational program needs and `realize` already runs: the checker does not import the generator; the stamp does not depend on the proposer; `UNKNOWN` is not green.

The physical rhyme is: **no privileged situated viewpoint.** The computational rhyme is: **no privileged proposer.** Same shape, different substrate.

## What v0 already is, in that language

`three_state` is the finite “every context consistent, no global section”:

- Three contexts (Ava / Ben / Cy).
- Every pair of contexts has a nonempty overlap.
- The triple overlap is empty.
- Pairwise yes is not a global section. The stamp is `UNSAT`, not a fluent compromise.

That is the discrete cousin of the continuous-selection fact: nonempty fibres pointwise do not give a global continuous operator (square-root on the circle). Gauge/diffeomorphism dressings live on the continuous side. This kernel stays discrete.

`UNKNOWN` is the other relational duty: do not invent a formula for a job you have not written.

## What would make this a DFM lattice adapter (not shipped)

A later semantics family, still fail-closed, still four verdicts:

| Spec | Candidate | Stamp |
|---|---|---|
| Finite graph, compact group, configuration | Dressing field + dressed observable | **C1** invariance of the dressed quantity |
| Choice of tree / reference | Same dressing rewritten on another tree | **C2** covariance: the certificate does not depend on the tree |
| Representation / encoding of contexts | Encoder | **C3** fibre adequacy: distinct relational facts are not collapsed |

Public finite plates, if that family is added:

1. U(1) on a lattice, tree dressing as the Lorenz-gauge analogue — C1/C2.
2. Abelian (or Higgs) matter dressing at a root, no SSB required — relational observable, not a vev.
3. `three_state` recast as above — C3, no global section.

Until that adapter exists, `realize check` does not compute a holonomy and does not certify section-independence of a dressed field.

## Firewall

Preferences, gauge choices, and proposer order may **search**. They may not rewrite C1–C3. An invariant path-integral paper that is “amenable to lattice implementations” still needs this layer: the lattice code proposes; an independent checker stamps.
